# Apply for Senior Citizen, Solo Parent, or PWD Assistance — City of Biñan

The City Government of Biñan, through the **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)**, ensures that vulnerable sectors receive robust financial, medical, and social support.

In 2026, the LGU introduced the **Biñan Access Plus Card ("One Card, One Family, All Access")** to streamline the delivery of these services, ensuring Biñanenses get priority access to city assistance.

---

## 1) Senior Citizen Programs (OSCA)

Registered senior citizens (60 years old and above) can access their benefits through the **Office for Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA)**.

**Key Local Benefits:**

- **Local Social Pension:** Biñan provides a localized pension of **₱500 per month (₱6,000 annually)** to qualified indigent senior citizens (separate from the national DSWD pension).
- **Cash Gifts:** The LGU issues a special one-time cash incentive of **₱5,000** to seniors reaching exactly 85 years of age.
- **Birthday Financial Gifts:** Distributed annually to all registered seniors.
- **Free Medicine & Healthcare:** Covered under the Biñan Access Plus Card for use at the Biñan City Hospital and local health centers.

**Requirements for OSCA ID & Pension:**

- Birth Certificate or any valid ID showing age (60+).
- Barangay Certificate of Residency (must be a registered voter and resident for at least two years to qualify for the local pension).
- 1x1 ID pictures.

---

## 2) Solo Parent Assistance

The CSWDO facilitates the issuance of the **Solo Parent ID** under the Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act (RA 11861).

**Key Local Benefits:**

- **Solo Parent Subsidy:** The city government has distributed localized subsidies (up to ₱6,000) to qualified indigent solo parents.
- 7 days parental leave (for employed parents).
- Prioritization in city scholarship programs and livelihood training.

**Requirements for Solo Parent ID:**

- Barangay Certificate of Residency.
- Proof of Financial Status (ITR, payslip, or Certificate of Indigency).
- PSA Birth Certificates of the child/children.
- Supporting documents proving solo parent status (e.g., death certificate of spouse, court order of separation, or affidavit of abandonment/separation for at least 12 months).

---

## 3) Persons with Disabilities (PDAO)

The **Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO)** issues the PWD ID and facilitates disability support.

**Key Local Benefits:**

- Standard 20% discount and VAT exemption on basic commodities, medicines, and transport.
- Financial birthday gifts distributed annually by the LGU.
- Prioritization in city hiring and SPES programs.

**Requirements for PWD ID:**

- Medical Certificate detailing the specific disability (from a licensed physician or the City Health Office).
- Barangay Certificate of Residency.
- 1x1 ID pictures.

---

## How to Apply

1. Visit the **CSWDO / OSCA / PDAO** offices located at the **Biñan City Hall Compound, Brgy. Zapote**.
2. Submit your complete requirements.
3. Fill out the application forms and undergo profiling for the **Biñan Access Plus Card** (if you have not yet secured one for your family).
