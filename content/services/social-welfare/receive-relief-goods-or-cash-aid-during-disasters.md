# Receive Relief Goods or Cash Aid During Disasters — City of Biñan

During severe typhoons, fires, or unforeseen calamities, the **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** works closely with the **CDRRMO** to provide immediate relief and financial assistance to affected Biñanenses.

---

## 1) Emergency Relief Goods (Food & Non-Food Items)

When preemptive or forced evacuation is implemented (e.g., during severe flooding of the Biñan River), the CSWDO activates its emergency welfare response.

- **What is provided:** Hot meals at evacuation centers, family food packs (rice, canned goods, noodles), and non-food items (sleeping mats, blankets, hygiene kits).
- **Where to claim:** Relief distribution is centralized at designated barangay evacuation centers (e.g., public schools, covered courts, or the Alonte Sports Arena for mass evacuations).
- **Requirement:** You must be logged in the **Evacuation Center Masterlist** managed by the barangay officials and CSWDO camp managers to receive relief packs.

---

## 2) Cash Assistance (AICS / Calamity Aid)

For families whose homes are severely damaged by fires, typhoons, or who are facing a sudden medical/financial crisis, cash aid is available.

**Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation (AICS):**

- Handled locally by the CSWDO and the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD).
- **For Fire/Calamity Victims:** The LGU provides localized cash assistance (Emergency Shelter Assistance) to help families rebuild.

**How to Apply for Crisis Cash Aid (Walk-in):**

1. Secure a **Certificate of Indigency** and a **Barangay Incident Report** (if a victim of fire or calamity).
2. Proceed to the **CSWDO** at City Hall for an intake interview with a social worker.
3. Submit required proofs (e.g., BFP Fire Report, photos of the damaged house, or medical abstracts if applying for medical cash aid).
4. The social worker will evaluate the documents and issue a Guarantee Letter or schedule a cash payout.

---

## 3) The Biñan Access Plus Card

Starting in 2026, the LGU utilizes the **Biñan Access Plus Card ("One Card, One Family, All Access")** to streamline the distribution of emergency relief. Presenting this card ensures that the city can quickly verify your residency and track the assistance provided to your family, preventing duplication and ensuring equitable distribution.
