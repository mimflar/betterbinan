# Join Livelihood or Skills Training Programs — City of Biñan

To promote economic resilience, the City Government offers free technical, vocational, and livelihood training through the **Public Employment Service Office (PESO)**, the **City Human Settlements and Livelihood Office**, and partner agencies like TESDA.

---

## 1) Technical and Vocational Training (TESDA-aligned)

The city regularly sponsors training for out-of-school youth (OSY), unemployed adults, and transitioning workers to help them gain employable skills and National Certificates (NC II).

**Common Courses Offered:**

- Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)
- Electrical Installation and Maintenance
- Cookery / Bread and Pastry Production
- Beauty Care and Hairdressing
- Computer Systems Servicing

**How to Join:**

- Follow the **PESO Biñan City** Facebook page for announcements regarding "Free Skills Training" intakes.
- Submit a Barangay Clearance, High School/College Diploma (or Form 138), Birth Certificate, and ID pictures to the PESO office at City Hall.

---

## 2) Community Livelihood Programs (For Women, Solo Parents, PWDs)

The **City Human Settlements and Livelihood Office** and the **CSWDO** run community-based livelihood programs designed for stay-at-home mothers, solo parents, and PWDs who wish to start home-based micro-businesses.

**Typical Workshops:**

- Soap and detergent making
- Meat processing (Longganisa, Tocino)
- Dressmaking and eco-bag sewing
- Urban gardening and food processing (in coordination with the City Agriculture Office)

**Capital Assistance:**
Graduates of these community training programs are often grouped into cooperatives or associations, making them eligible to apply for **starter kits** or Livelihood Assistance Grants (LAG) from the city or the DSWD.
