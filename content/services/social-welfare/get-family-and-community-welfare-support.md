# Get Family and Community Welfare Support — City of Biñan

The **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** provides psychosocial interventions, counseling, and legal protective services to safeguard the well-being of families and children in Biñan.

---

## 1) Protection Against Violence and Abuse (VAWC)

The city strictly enforces Republic Act 9262 (Anti-Violence Against Women and Their Children Act) and child protection laws.

- **Who is protected:** Women and children suffering from physical, emotional, psychological, or economic abuse.
- **Where to go:** Victims should immediately report to the **Barangay VAWC Desk** (available in all Biñan barangays) or the **Women and Children Protection Desk (WCPD)** at the Biñan PNP Station.
- **LGU Intervention:** The CSWDO provides temporary shelter, psychosocial counseling, and legal assistance in coordination with the City Legal Office to file Protection Orders against abusers.

## 2) Family and Pre-Marriage Counseling

- **Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counseling (PMOC):** Couples intending to get married must attend this mandatory seminar conducted jointly by the **City Population Office**, the **City Health Office**, and the **CSWDO**. It covers family planning, maternal health, and responsible parenthood.
- **Family Counseling:** Families experiencing severe domestic conflict, or parents dealing with Children in Conflict with the Law (CICL), can request mediation and professional counseling sessions from licensed CSWDO social workers.

## 3) Child and Youth Welfare Programs

- **Children in Conflict with the Law (CICL):** Minors who commit offenses are not thrown into regular jails. The CSWDO handles their diversion programs, providing rehabilitation, values formation, and safe halfway homes to reintegrate them into society.
- **Child Abuse Reporting:** If you suspect a child in your neighborhood is being neglected, physically abused, or exploited, report it immediately to the CSWDO or the Biñan Police. The identity of the reporter will be kept strictly confidential.
