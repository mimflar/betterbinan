# Attend Farming or Fishing Training — City of Biñan

The **City Agriculture Office**, in partnership with the Department of Agriculture (DA), Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR), and the City Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Management Council (CFARMC), conducts regular capacity-building seminars for Biñan's agricultural sector.

---

## Types of Training Programs

- **Urban and Container Gardening:** Techniques for growing vegetables in small spaces using recycled materials. Ideal for households and schools.
- **Good Agricultural Practices (GAP):** Seminars for farmers on proper pesticide use, soil management, and maximizing crop yield.
- **Aquaculture & Fishery Management:** Training for coastal barangays along Laguna de Bay on sustainable fishing practices, fish processing, and fingerling rearing.
- **Livestock Management:** Conducted jointly with the City Veterinary Office for backyard swine and poultry raisers on biosecurity and disease prevention (e.g., African Swine Fever awareness).

## How to Join

**1. Stay Updated:**

- Training schedules are regularly announced via the **Biñan City Information Office** Facebook page and through local Barangay Agricultural Workers.
  **2. Register through your Barangay:**
- Most community training sessions require pre-registration at your local Barangay Hall to secure a slot.
  **3. Request a Seminar (For Groups):**
- Cooperatives, homeowners' associations, or schools can formally request customized training. Submit a letter of request to the **Head of the City Agriculture Office**, indicating the desired topic and estimated number of participants.
