# Borrow Farm Tools or Equipment — City of Biñan

To aid local farmers in land preparation and harvesting, the **City Agriculture Office** maintains a pool of agricultural machinery and equipment that can be borrowed or scheduled for use.

---

## Available Equipment

Depending on current inventory and maintenance schedules, registered farmers can request the use of:

- Hand tractors (Kuliglig)
- Water pumps (for irrigation during dry spells)
- Grass cutters
- Spraying equipment (for organic or safe pesticide application)

## Who Can Borrow

- Individual farmers registered under the **RSBSA**.
- Recognized Farmers' Associations and Agricultural Cooperatives in Biñan.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Borrowing

1. **Verify Availability**
   - Call or visit the **City Agriculture Office** well in advance of your target date to check if the machinery is available and functional.
2. **Submit a Request**
   - Fill out the **Equipment Request/Borrower's Slip**.
   - You must present your RSBSA ID or a certification from your Farmers' Association.
3. **Approval and Briefing**
   - The Agricultural Technician will approve the request and provide a brief orientation on the safe operation and maintenance of the equipment.
   - _Note: For heavy machinery, the city may dispatch an official operator along with the equipment rather than lending it directly to the farmer._
4. **Return and Reporting**
   - Return the equipment on the agreed date in a clean and functional state. Report any mechanical issues immediately to the office so they can be repaired for the next user.
