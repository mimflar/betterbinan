# Veterinary Services for Livestock and Pets — City of Biñan

The **Biñan City Veterinary Office**, headed by Dr. Donovan V. Marcellana, provides comprehensive animal health services to prevent zoonotic diseases, support livestock raisers, and promote responsible pet ownership.

---

## Services Offered

**For Livestock and Poultry (Swine, Cattle, Goats, Chickens):**

- Disease surveillance and blood collection
- Vaccination and deworming
- Vitamin supplementation
- Technical assistance for backyard raisers
- Meat Inspection (at the City Slaughterhouse to ensure meat sold in public markets is safe)

**For Companion Animals (Dogs & Cats):**

- Free Anti-Rabies Vaccination
- Spay and Neuter (Kapon) Medical Missions
- Basic consultation and treatment

## How to Avail (Walk-in & Field Services)

**For Pet Owners (Walk-in):**

1. Bring your pet to the **City Veterinary Office**.
2. Ensure dogs are leashed and cats are in secure carriers.
3. Present your valid ID showing Biñan residency.
4. The veterinarian will administer the anti-rabies vaccine or conduct a check-up and issue a Vaccination Card.

**For Livestock Raisers (Field Visit Request):**

1. Call the City Veterinary Office or visit in person to request a site visit.
2. Provide your farm address, the number of animals, and the specific concern (e.g., routine deworming or suspected illness).
3. The city vet team will schedule a field visit to your backyard farm.
