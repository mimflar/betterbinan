# Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers, and Fingerlings — City of Biñan

The **City Agriculture Office**, headed by Antonio P. Aguilar, supports local farmers, fisherfolk, and urban gardeners in Biñan by distributing free agricultural inputs to sustain food security and local livelihood.

---

## What is Available

- **Vegetable Seeds & Seedlings:** Pechay, string beans (sitaw), eggplant, tomato, squash, and okra (often distributed for urban gardening or the _Gulayan sa Barangay/Paaralan_ programs).
- **Fertilizers:** Organic and synthetic fertilizers for registered rice and high-value crop farmers.
- **Fingerlings:** Tilapia and bangus fingerlings for local fisherfolk operating in the Laguna de Bay area (in coordination with CFARMC and Bantay Lawa).

## Who Can Apply

- **Registered Farmers and Fisherfolk:** Must be registered in the **Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA)**.
- **Urban Gardeners & Cooperatives:** Homeowners' associations, public schools, and community groups promoting urban agriculture.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Visit the City Agriculture Office**
   - Located at the Biñan City Hall Compound.
   - Bring your RSBSA ID or a valid ID proving Biñan residency.
2. **Submit a Request**
   - For individual urban gardeners: Fill out the request form at the desk. Seeds are usually given on the same day if in stock.
   - For farmers/fisherfolk: Present your RSBSA ID. The office will verify your farm/pond location and allocate fertilizers or fingerlings based on your production area.
3. **Receive Inputs & Instructions**
   - Agricultural technicians will provide the materials along with basic instructions on proper planting/feeding techniques.
