# Enroll Children in LGU Daycare or Preschool Programs — City of Biñan

The City Government of Biñan, through the **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** and local barangays, operates **Child Development Centers (Daycare)** and supports **public preschool education**.

---

## 1) LGU Child Development Centers (Daycare)

**What’s offered**

- Early childhood care and development for children aged **3 to 4 years old**.
- Daily activities focusing on socialization, basic learning skills, and values formation.
- Supplementary feeding programs for enrolled children to monitor health and nutrition.

**Who can join**

- Children aged **3–4 years old** who are residents of the specific barangay.
- Priority is often given to indigent families.

**Requirements (typical)**

- Child’s **Birth Certificate** (PSA or Local Civil Registrar copy).
- **Barangay Clearance** or proof of residency of the parents.
- 1×1 ID photo of the child.
- Filled-out **Daycare Enrollment Form** (available at the center).

**How to enroll**

1. Visit your **Barangay Hall** or the local **Child Development Center** (usually located near the barangay hall/covered court).
2. Ask for the enrollment schedule (typically during the summer months of May–July).
3. Submit the required documents and attend the mandatory parents' orientation.

**Cost:** Free (LGU-funded).

---

## 2) Public Preschool (DepEd Kindergarten)

**What’s offered**

- Department of Education (DepEd) **Kindergarten Program** for **5-year-old children**, implemented in all public elementary schools in Biñan.
- Focuses on formal school readiness: literacy, numeracy, and motor skills.

**Who can join**

- Children who will turn **5 years old** by the opening of the school year.
- Residents within the public school’s geographical catchment area.

**Requirements (typical DepEd)**

- Child’s **Birth Certificate** (PSA).
- Proof of residency (Barangay Certificate).
- 2×2 ID photos.
- Accomplished **DepEd Kindergarten Registration Form**.

**How to enroll**

1. Wait for the **DepEd Early Registration** period (usually announced between January and March on the DepEd Tayo Biñan City page).
2. Proceed to the **nearest public elementary school**.
3. Submit the required documents to the school registrar or designated early registration desk.

**Cost:** Free (National Government-funded).
