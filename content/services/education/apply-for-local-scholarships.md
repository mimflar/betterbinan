# Apply for Local Scholarships — City of Biñan

Use this guide to find **City/LGU**, **Congressional**, **local college**, and **national** scholarship options you can apply for while based in the **City of Biñan**.

---

## 1) City/LGU Scholarship Programs

| Program                           | Who it’s for                                                                                        | What it covers                     | Where to apply                                  | Notes                                                                                                                                                              |
| :-------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Iskolar ng Biñan (INB)**        | Biñan City residents (College level; priority to low-income households with good academic standing) | Tuition assistance and/or stipends | City Hall / Youth and Sports Development Office | The flagship scholarship program of the city. Watch for the official application period announced via the CIO Facebook page before the start of the academic year. |
| **Student-Athlete & Arts Grants** | Students representing Biñan in regional/national sports or arts competitions                        | Cash allowances / stipends         | Mayor's Office / BCHATO / Sports Office         | Championed by Mayor Gel Alonte to foster discipline and excellence among the youth.                                                                                |

## 2) Congressional / Partner Scholarship Slots

| Program                                     | Who it’s for                  | What it covers                    | Where to apply            | Notes                                                                                                                          |

## 3) Local Colleges & Their In-House Scholarships

| School                                                 | Scholarship page/info                                | Notes                                                                                                                                |
| :----------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PUP - Biñan Campus**                                 | Apply via PUP system guidelines                      | A state university heavily supported by the Biñan LGU. Tuition is free under the Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act. |
| **University of Perpetual Help System Laguna (UPHSL)** | Public scholarship lists at the Registrar/Admissions | Offers academic, varsity, and alumni discounts.                                                                                      |
| **Trimex Colleges**                                    | Inquire at their Admissions Office                   | Local private college offering institutional grants.                                                                                 |

> Tip: Even if you’re targeting an **LGU scholarship**, apply to your target school’s **in-house scholarship** too.

## 4) National Programs You Can Apply For

| Program                           | Who it’s for                                        | Typical coverage                           | Where to file                              |
| :-------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- |
| **CHED Merit Scholarship (CMSP)** | Incoming college with high grades + income criteria | Stipend + tuition assistance               | CHED Regional Office 4A / CHED portal      |
| **DOST-SEI Scholarship**          | STEM track Grade 12 students                        | Tuition + monthly stipend + book allowance | DOST portal; often promoted by DepEd Biñan |
| **OWWA EDSP/ODSP**                | Dependents of active OFWs                           | EDSP up to ₱60k/yr; ODSP up to ₱20k/yr     | OWWA Region 4A / PESO Biñan                |

---

## Common Requirements (Have these ready)

- **Proof of residency** (Barangay Certificate / Voter's ID of parent)
- **Birth certificate** (PSA)
- **Report card/grades** (Form 138 / Transcript of Records)
- **Good moral certificate** (from school)
- **Income documents** (Parents’ ITR or Certificate of Indigency from the Barangay)
- **Admission/Enrollment proof** (Certificate of Registration/Enrollment)

---

## Step-by-Step: How to Apply

1. **Pick your target programs** (City LGU, school-based, and national).
2. **Check the current call** via the Biñan CIO or DepEd Biñan pages.
3. **Assemble documents** using the checklist above.
4. **Submit** to the specified office (City Hall, CHED, or your school).
5. **Comply every term** by maintaining the required GWA and submitting your grades on time.
