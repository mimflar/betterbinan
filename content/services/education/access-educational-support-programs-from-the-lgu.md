# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU — City of Biñan

Below are **city-led** or **LGU-facilitated** programs you can use for study support: free school supplies, public library access, student employment (SPES) via PESO, scholarships, and cash/education assistance.

---

## 1) Free School Supplies (LGU & SEF-funded)

**What it is**

- The City Government provides **free school supplies, bags, and uniforms** to public school learners, announced each school year prior to the opening of classes.

**How to access**

1. Watch your school’s advisory and the **DepEd Tayo Biñan City** Facebook page for **distribution schedules**.
2. Learners (with parent/guardian) claim supplies at their respective public schools on the designated date.

---

## 2) Biñan City Public Library (Study Space & Resources)

**What it is**

- A **government-run public library** offering study spaces, reference materials, and computer access for students. The city also integrates cultural and historical learning through the **Biñan City Culture, History, Arts and Tourism Office (BCHATO)**.

**How to access**

1. Visit the public library located near the **Old Municipal Building / Plaza Rizal**.
2. Present your valid Student ID to sign the logbook and access the facilities.

---

## 3) Student Employment (SPES) via PESO Biñan

**What it is**

- The **Special Program for the Employment of Students (SPES)** lets poor but deserving **students/OSYs (15–30)** work during summer breaks at City Hall or partner private companies to earn their tuition.

**How to access**

1. Follow **PESO Biñan City** on Facebook for **SPES application windows** (usually posted around February to March).
2. Prepare your PSA Birth Certificate, latest grades, and parents' Certificate of Indigency/Low Income.
3. Submit online or at the PESO office (City Hall Compound); attend the screening and orientation.

---

## 4) LGU-linked Scholarships & Incentives

**What it is**

- The City operates the **Iskolar ng Biñan (INB)** program, while the Congressional office periodically opens national educational assistance slots.

**How to access**

1. Monitor the **Biñan City Information Office (CIO)** and the **Office of Cong. Len Alonte** for official calls.
2. Prepare residency and academic documents; submit by the deadline to the Youth and Sports Development Office or City Hall.

---

## 5) Educational / Cash Assistance (DSWD / LGU)

**What it is**

- **Educational assistance pay-outs** (like AICS) are nationally run by DSWD but are coordinated locally with the **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)**.

**How to access**

1. Watch announcements from **DSWD-4A** and the **Biñan CIO** for payout schedules.
2. Bring your Student ID, Certificate of Enrollment, and a Barangay Certificate of Residency on your assigned date.

---

## Quick Directory

- **Biñan City Information Office (CIO)** — city-wide program announcements
- **PESO Biñan City** — SPES, job fairs, recruitment & youth work programs
- **DepEd Division of Biñan City** — school memos and supply distributions
- **CSWDO Biñan** — coordination for DSWD educational cash assistance
