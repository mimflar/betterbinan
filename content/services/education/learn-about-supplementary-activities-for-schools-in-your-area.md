# Supplementary Activities for Schools — City of Biñan

This guide shows where to find **clubs, student organizations, arts programs, and civic groups** available to learners in the City of Biñan.

---

## Where to Look First (Local)

1. **Your school’s official Facebook page** — most clubs post tryout/enlistment dates here.
2. **DepEd Tayo Biñan City** — for division-wide contests, sports meets, and official memorandums.
3. **BCHATO (Biñan City Culture, History, Arts and Tourism Office)** — the LGU's hub for heritage tours, youth theater, and traditional dance troupes (like the Biñan Folkloric Dance Troupe).

## Arts, Culture, and Sports (Biñan Specialties)

- **BCHATO Youth Programs:** Biñan takes massive pride in its history (e.g., Jose Rizal's early education in Biñan). Students can join LGU-sponsored theater workshops, heritage guiding, and arts month competitions.
- **Sports Clinics:** The Biñan City Sports Office regularly conducts free summer sports clinics (basketball, volleyball, taekwondo, chess) at the Alonte Sports Arena and barangay covered courts.

---

## How to Join (Quick Steps)

1. **Scan your school’s bulletin/FB** during the first month of classes for club fairs.
2. **Prepare simple requirements** (School ID, parent consent form).
3. **Attend the orientation/tryouts** for sports, journalism, or performing arts.
4. **Follow the City Government Facebook Pages** (CIO, BCHATO, Youth and Sports Development) to catch open registrations for city-wide summer programs.
