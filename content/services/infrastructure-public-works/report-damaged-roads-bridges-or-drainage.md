# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges, or Drainage — City of Biñan

Maintaining safe infrastructure is a shared priority. The **City Engineering Office** handles the repair and maintenance of local city and barangay roads, while national highways (like the Old National Highway and Manila South Road) fall under the jurisdiction of the **DPWH Laguna II District Engineering Office**.

---

## 1) What to Report

- **Potholes or Damaged Pavement:** Deep cracks or sinkholes on barangay or city roads.
- **Clogged or Collapsed Drainage:** Flooding caused by blocked canals, broken manhole covers, or collapsed esteros.
- **Streetlights & Electrical:** Busted city streetlights or dangling utility wires posing a public hazard.
- **Bridge Structural Issues:** Visible cracks or washouts near bridges and spillways over the Biñan River.

## 2) How to Report (City and Barangay Roads)

For local streets and subdivisions, the LGU is the direct responder.

1. **Document the Damage:** Take clear photos showing the damage and its surroundings.
2. **Note the Exact Location:** Record the street name, barangay, and the nearest landmark or house number.
3. **Submit the Report:**
   - **Through the Barangay:** Proceed to your Barangay Hall and report it to the **Committee on Public Works / Infrastructure**. The barangay will forward it to the City Engineering Office for funding and repair.
   - **Direct to the City:** Message the official **Biñan City Information Office - CIO** Facebook page with the photos and location, or call the City Engineering Office at City Hall.

## 3) Immediate Hazards (Emergency)

If a collapsed road, massive sinkhole, or fallen electrical post completely blocks traffic or poses an immediate threat to life:

- Call the **Biñan Command and Control Center (C3) Emergency Hotline:** **0917-120-8911** or **(049) 536-2622**.
- C3 will immediately dispatch the **CDRRMO** to secure the area and the **City Engineering Office** or **MERALCO** for emergency clearing.
