# Rent Market Stalls and Use LGU Cemeteries — City of Biñan

The city manages vital economic and public welfare facilities, ensuring residents have access to regulated trading spaces and affordable burial grounds.

---

## 1) Rent Stalls at the Biñan Public Market

The Biñan Public Market is one of the busiest trading hubs in the city, offering spaces for wet goods, dry goods, and local delicacies.

**How to Apply for a Stall:**

1. **Inquire on Vacancy:** Visit the **Office of the City Market Administrator** (located within the public market) to check for available stalls.
2. **Submit Application:** Provide a Letter of Intent, Barangay Clearance, Voter’s ID (Biñan residents are prioritized), and 2x2 photos.
3. **Approval:** The City Market Committee reviews the application. Once approved, you will pay the advance rental and deposit to the **City Treasurer’s Office**.
4. **Permits:** After signing the lease contract, you must secure a Sanitary Permit (Health Office) and a Mayor’s/Business Permit (BPLO).
   _Note: Subleasing (selling of rights) is strictly prohibited._

---

## 2) Biñan Public Cemetery Services

The LGU manages the **Biñan Public Cemetery** to provide accessible and orderly burial services for its residents, especially indigent families.

**Services Offered:**

- Leasing of apartment-style niches (tombs) for a specific number of years.
- Renewal of leases for existing tombs.
- Exhumation and bone transfer services.

**How to Process a Burial:**

1. **Death Certificate:** Ensure the Death Certificate is duly registered with the **Local Civil Registry (LCR)** at City Hall.
2. **Secure Permits:** Proceed to the **City Health Office** to secure a Burial Permit and a Transfer Permit (if the deceased is coming from outside Biñan).
3. **Cemetery Administration:** Present the Death and Burial Permits to the **Cemetery Administrator's Office**.
4. **Payment:** Pay the required lease or burial fees at the City Treasurer's Office.
5. **Indigent Assistance:** If the family cannot afford the burial fees, they may request a Certificate of Indigency from their barangay and apply for Burial Assistance through the **CSWDO** or the Mayor's Office.

**Contact Information:**

- **Biñan Public Cemetery:** Located near the DepEd Schools Division Office and the old Biñan train station.
- **Local Civil Registry:** City Hall Compound, Brgy. Zapote
