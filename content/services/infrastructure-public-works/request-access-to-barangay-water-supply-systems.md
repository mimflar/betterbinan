# Request Water Connection and Supply — City of Biñan

Access to clean and potable water in Biñan is primarily managed by **Laguna Water (Laguna AAA Water Corporation)**, a joint venture between the Provincial Government of Laguna and Manila Water, alongside localized subdivision/barangay waterworks.

---

## 1) Laguna Water Connection (Main Provider)

For most barangays, commercial establishments, and industrial zones (like Laguna Technopark) in Biñan, Laguna Water is the official provider.

**Requirements for New Residential Connection:**

- Duly accomplished Service Application Form.
- Proof of Ownership (Land Title, Tax Declaration, or Deed of Sale) or Notarized Lease Contract with the owner's consent.
- Photocopy of 2 Valid Government IDs.
- Barangay Clearance indicating your exact address.
- Latest picture of the property where the meter will be installed.

**How to Apply:**

1. Submit your requirements at the **Laguna Water Business Center**.
2. A field representative will conduct a site inspection to determine the distance from the main pipeline and compute the piping materials needed.
3. Pay the installation and guarantee deposit fees.
4. The meter will be installed and water service activated within the committed timeframe.

**Contact Laguna Water:**

- **Customer Service Hotline:** 0998-559-2306 (Smart) / 0998-559-2469 (Text)
- **Email:** info@lagunawater.com.ph
- **Website:** www.lagunawater.com.ph

---

## 2) Subdivision / Barangay Waterworks

In some older subdivisions or specific barangays, water is supplied by an independent Homeowners Association (HOA) pump or a Barangay Waterworks System.

**How to apply:**

1. Visit your **HOA Office** or **Barangay Hall**.
2. Request a water tapping permit.
3. Pay the local association's connection fee. Maintenance and monthly billing are handled directly by the HOA/Barangay rather than Laguna Water.

---

## 3) Report Water Interruptions or Leaks

If you notice a major pipe burst flooding the street, or if you experience an unannounced water interruption:

- Contact **Laguna Water's hotline (0998-559-2306)** immediately with the exact location of the leak to prevent water waste and road damage.
- Follow the **Laguna Water** Facebook page for official announcements regarding scheduled maintenance and service advisories in Biñan.
