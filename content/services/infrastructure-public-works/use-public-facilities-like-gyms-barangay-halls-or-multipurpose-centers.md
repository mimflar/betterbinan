# Use Public Facilities (Gyms, Covered Courts, and Plazas) — City of Biñan

The City Government of Biñan maintains numerous public facilities for sports, community assemblies, and cultural events. These include the premier Alonte Sports Arena, the Historic Plaza Rizal, and various barangay covered courts.

---

## 1) Barangay Covered Courts and Multipurpose Halls

Every barangay in Biñan has a covered court used for basketball, community meetings, medical missions, and private events (like birthdays or wakes).

**How to Reserve:**

1. Visit your **Barangay Hall** and check the calendar of events with the Barangay Secretary or the Kagawad in charge of Sports/Infrastructure.
2. Submit a formal letter of request specifying the date, time, and purpose of the event.
3. Pay the minimal maintenance/electricity fee (varies per barangay).
4. **Rules:** Clean as you go (CLAYGO) is strictly enforced. Organizers are responsible for any damages to the facility.

---

## 2) Alonte Sports Arena

Located in Brgy. Zapote (near City Hall), this is the city's premier indoor arena, used for PBA games, national sports tournaments, graduation ceremonies, and large-scale city events.

**How to Reserve (For Large Organizations/Events):**

1. Usage of the Alonte Sports Arena requires higher-level approval due to its scale and maintenance costs.
2. Submit a formal letter of intent addressed to the **City Mayor** and the **City Administrator's Office**.
3. State the nature of the event, expected crowd size, and specific logistical needs (e.g., lighting, VIP rooms).
4. The City Administrator’s Office will assess the request and issue the corresponding rental fees and guidelines.

---

## 3) Plaza Rizal and Heritage Sites

For cultural shoots, school historical tours, or civic ceremonies at the Plaza Rizal or the School of Dr. Jose P. Rizal Site and Museum.

**How to Reserve:**

- Coordinate directly with the **Biñan City Culture, History, Arts and Tourism Office (BCHATO)**.
- **Location:** Old Municipal Building, Plaza Rizal.
- Follow their guidelines to ensure the preservation of these historical landmarks during your activity.
