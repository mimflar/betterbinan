# Rent Stalls in Public Markets — City of Biñan

Use this guide to lease commercial space, stalls, or kiosks in the **Biñan Public Market** and other LGU-managed trading centers.

---

## Who Can Rent

- **Biñan City Residents** (often given priority for stall awarding)
- **Local MSMEs and Vendors** (selling dry goods, wet market goods, groceries, or food)
- **Cooperatives and Livelihood Associations**

_Note: Stall availability is strictly subject to vacancy. The awarding of stalls is usually governed by the City Market Committee._

---

## What You’ll Need (Typical Requirements)

**Application Documents**

- **Letter of Intent** addressed to the City Mayor and the City Market Administrator.
- **Duly filled-out Application Form** (available at the Market Office).
- **Barangay Clearance** (from your barangay of residence).
- **Voter’s ID or Voter's Certification** (to prove Biñan City residency).
- **Recent 2x2 ID pictures**.

**Post-Approval/Operations Documents**

- **Health Certificate / Sanitary Permit** (from the City Health Office, strictly required for wet market, meat, fish, and food vendors).
- **Mayor’s / Business Permit** (processed after the lease is approved).

---

## Step-by-Step (Walk-In)

1. **Inquire on Vacancy**
   - Visit the **Office of the City Market Administrator** (usually located within the Biñan Public Market premises) to check for available stalls in your desired section (e.g., Fish, Meat, Vegetables, Dry Goods, Eatery).

2. **Submit Application**
   - Submit your **Letter of Intent** and required documents to the Market Administrator's Office.
   - Your application will be reviewed by the **City Market Committee**, which evaluates applicants based on local ordinances and residency priority.

3. **Approval and Assessment**
   - Once approved, you will receive a Notice of Award or approval document.
   - The office will assess your initial fees, which typically include an advance rental fee, a deposit, and sometimes a goodwill fee (depending on the stall classification).

4. **Payment of Fees**
   - Proceed to the **City Treasurer’s Office** (or the designated market collector) to pay the required fees.
   - Always secure an **Official Receipt (OR)**.

5. **Contract Signing**
   - Sign the **Contract of Lease** with the City Government. Ensure you read the market rules regarding operating hours, sanitation, and subleasing (which is strictly prohibited).

6. **Process Business Permit**
   - With your lease contract secured, proceed to the **BPLO** at the City Hall Compound to process your **Mayor's / Business Permit** before officially opening your stall.

---

## Important Market Rules to Remember

- **No Subleasing:** Renters are strictly prohibited from subleasing or selling their stall rights to a third party. Violations result in immediate revocation of the lease.
- **Sanitation:** Vendors must maintain cleanliness in their immediate vicinity. Food and wet market vendors must wear proper attire (aprons, hairnets) and possess updated Health Cards.
- **Daily Tickets/Rentals:** Market fees/rentals are often collected daily by authorized collectors from the City Treasurer's Office. Always ask for your daily cash tickets or OR.
- **Weights and Measures:** All weighing scales must be registered, calibrated, and sealed annually by the City Treasurer's Office to protect consumers.
