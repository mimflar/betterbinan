# Access Maternal Care & Child Immunization — City of Biñan

The Biñan City Health Office (CHO) and local barangay health centers provide **prenatal care**, **family planning**, and **free routine child immunization** for residents to ensure safe pregnancies and healthy infants.

---

## Maternal Care (Prenatal & Related Services)

| Service                                                                  | Where                   | Schedule                                         | Requirements                                             | Cost                      |
| :----------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------ |
| **Prenatal check-ups** (by midwife/nurse; OB-GYN referral for high-risk) | Barangay Health Centers | Mon–Fri (typical clinic hours; varies by center) | Prenatal record / Maternity Book (issued at first visit) | **Free**                  |
| **Lying-in Services** (Normal spontaneous delivery)                      | City Lying-in Clinics   | 24/7                                             | PhilHealth ID / MDR, complete prenatal records           | **Covered by PhilHealth** |
| **Family Planning counseling & methods**                                 | Barangay Health Centers | Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM                         | Personal appearance for counseling                       | **Free**                  |

**Tip:** Bring any previous prenatal card/records, ultrasound results, and a valid ID. High-risk pregnancies will be referred directly to the Biñan City Hospital.

---

## Child Immunization (EPI)

Routine childhood vaccines are provided **free** under the Department of Health's Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI).

| Age / Visit                            | Vaccines                                                                 | Where                   | Regular Day / Time                                                       | Requirements                                      | Cost     |
| :------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------ | :------- |
| **Birth–12 months** (per DOH schedule) | **BCG**, **Pentavalent**, **OPV/IPV**, **MMR**, **Hepatitis B**, **PCV** | Barangay Health Centers | Designated "Bakuna Day" (usually Wed or Thurs, check with your barangay) | **Baby Book / ECCD card** (issued on first visit) | **Free** |

> **First time?** The health worker will issue a Baby Book (Mother and Child Book) and write down your return dates for the next vaccine doses.

---

## How to Avail (Quick Steps)

1. **Go to your Barangay Health Center** during clinic hours (or the posted EPI Bakuna Day for vaccines).
2. **Register/Update your record:** Prenatal record for mothers, **Baby Book** for children (issued if first time).
3. **Receive service:** Prenatal assessment, counseling (and TT vaccine/supplements), or child vaccination per EPI schedule.
4. **Book your return date** for follow-ups and next vaccine doses as advised by the midwife or health worker.

---

## Notes & Extras

- **Medical consultations** for children (pediatrics) at health centers are **free**.
- Always bring your **PhilHealth ID or Member Data Record (MDR)**, as this helps the city health facilities process coverage for facility-based deliveries and newborn care packages.
