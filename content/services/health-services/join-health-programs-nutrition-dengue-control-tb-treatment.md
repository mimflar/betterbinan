# Join Health Programs (Nutrition, Dengue Control, TB Treatment) — City of Biñan

Below are city-led and national programs you can join through your **Barangay Health Center** and the **Biñan City Health Office (CHO)**.

---

## 1) Nutrition Programs

**What’s offered**

- **Operation Timbang Plus (OPT):** Annual weighing and measuring of children under 5 years old.
- **Supplementary Feeding:** For malnourished or underweight children identified during OPT (90 to 120-day feeding programs).
- Special events during Nutrition Month (July) and city-wide wellness programs.

**How to join**

1. Visit your **Barangay Health Center** or ask the **Barangay Nutrition Scholar (BNS)** about the Operation Timbang schedule.
2. If your child qualifies, the BNS will enroll them in the barangay's supplementary feeding program.

**Cost:** Free for eligible beneficiaries.

---

## 2) Dengue Control Programs

**What’s offered**

- Implementation of the **4S Strategy** (Search & Destroy, Self-protection, Seek early consultation, Say yes to fogging during outbreaks).
- Free NS1 Dengue rapid testing for patients experiencing high fever and joint pains.
- Targeted misting, fogging, and larvicide application led by the CHO.

**How to join**

1. Take part in barangay clean-up drives (searching and destroying stagnant water).
2. **For Fogging Requests:** If there is a clustering of cases in your subdivision or street, your Homeowners Association (HOA) or Barangay Captain must submit a formal request to the CHO for misting.

**Cost:** Free; NS1 tests are available at health centers.

---

## 3) TB Treatment (TB-DOTS)

**What’s offered**

- Free consultations, sputum testing, and GeneXpert diagnostics.
- Full course of anti-TB medicines under the **Directly Observed Treatment, Short-course (DOTS)** approach.

**Where**

- Biñan City Health Office (Main DOTS Clinic) and affiliated Barangay Health Centers.

**How to join**

1. Go to your **Barangay Health Center** or the **CHO** immediately if you experience a cough lasting more than 2 weeks, unexplained weight loss, or night sweats.
2. Submit a sputum sample for testing.
3. If diagnosed, you will be enrolled in DOTS — 6 months of daily treatment and medicines are given for free, monitored by a health worker.

**Cost:** Free for all TB patients.
