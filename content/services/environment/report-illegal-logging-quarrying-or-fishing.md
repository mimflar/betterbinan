# Report Illegal Environmental Activities — City of Biñan

Protecting Biñan’s natural resources—especially the Laguna de Bay and local waterways—requires active community vigilance. The LGU strictly enforces environmental laws against illegal fishing, illegal logging, and improper waste disposal.

---

## 1) Reporting Illegal Fishing in Laguna de Bay

Illegal fishing practices (such as the use of fine-mesh nets, dynamite, or unauthorized fish pens) destroy the lake's ecosystem and harm the livelihood of legitimate fisherfolk.

- **Who responds:** The **Biñan City Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Management Council (CFARMC)** and the **Bantay Lawa** (Lake Wardens), operating under the City Agriculture Office and the Laguna Lake Development Authority (LLDA).
- **How to report:**
  1. Note the exact location (nearby barangay or landmark) and the time of the incident.
  2. Take photos or videos if it is safe to do so.
  3. Report immediately to the **City Agriculture Office** at (049) 513-5031, or directly to your local Barangay Hall in coastal areas (e.g., Brgy. Malaban, Brgy. Dela Paz).

---

## 2) Reporting Illegal Logging, Dumping, or Earth-moving

Unauthorized cutting of trees (even on private property), illegal dumping of toxic/solid waste into the Biñan River, or unregulated earth-moving activities must be stopped immediately.

- **Who responds:** The **CENRO**, the **DENR-PENRO Satellite Office**, and the **Public Order and Safety Office (POSO)**.
- **How to report:**
  1. Do not confront the violators directly to ensure your safety.
  2. Record the vehicle plate numbers (if trucks are involved in illegal dumping), the exact address, and a description of the activity.
  3. Call the **CENRO Hotline** at (049) 513-5096 / 523-5409.
  4. For immediate intervention during off-hours, contact the **Biñan Command and Control Center (C3)** at 0917-120-8911 or the **Biñan PNP** at (049) 513-5111.

---

## What Happens After You Report?

1. **Verification:** CENRO inspectors or Bantay Lawa patrollers will be dispatched to verify the complaint.
2. **Issuance of Violation:** If caught, the LGU will issue a Notice of Violation, confiscate illegal equipment (like fishnets or chainsaws), and impose fines based on the local Environmental Code.
3. **Escalation:** Severe violations are escalated to the **DENR** or the **Laguna Lake Development Authority (LLDA)** for the filing of criminal charges.
