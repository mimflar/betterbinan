# Join Clean-up Drives and Tree Planting — City of Biñan

The **City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)**, alongside various civic organizations, actively pursues the rehabilitation of the Biñan River, the Laguna de Bay coastline, and the greening of urban spaces.

---

## 1) River and Coastal Clean-ups

Because Biñan is a coastal city along Laguna de Bay and is intersected by the Biñan River, clean-up drives are critical for flood prevention and water quality.

**Types of Activities:**

- **Biñan River Clean-ups:** Often held in coordination with private partners (like the SMC Better Rivers PH initiative) to extract silt and solid waste from barangays like Poblacion and Malaban.
- **Coastal Clean-ups (Laguna Lake):** Focused on removing discarded fish nets, plastics, and water hyacinths along the lakeshore barangays.

**Who can join:**

- Student organizations (YES-O, Boy/Girl Scouts)
- Homeowners' Associations (HOAs)
- Corporate volunteers and civic groups (e.g., Lions Club, Rotary)

**How to join or organize:**

- Watch for announcements on the **Biñan City Information Office** Facebook page for city-wide clean-up schedules.
- If your group wants to organize your own clean-up drive, coordinate with **CENRO** to secure garbage collection trucks and proper disposal routing after the activity.

---

## 2) Urban Tree Planting Programs

To maintain the city's green spaces and comply with national greening programs, the LGU regularly conducts tree planting activities.

**Where it happens:**

- Public school grounds, open subdivision spaces, and designated eco-parks.
- Rehabilitation areas along riverbanks.

**How to request seedlings:**

- Civic groups and HOAs can formally request free seedlings from the **City Agriculture Office** or **CENRO** for community planting.
- Submit a Letter of Request specifying the planting location, the number of volunteers, and the commitment to maintain and water the saplings.
