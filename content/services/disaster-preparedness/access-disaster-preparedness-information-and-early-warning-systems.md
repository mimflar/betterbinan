# Access Early Warning Systems and Disaster Info — City of Biñan

Timely information is your best defense against natural hazards. Biñan City utilizes multiple channels to disseminate weather updates, flood warnings, and class suspensions.

---

## Official Information Channels

For official updates directly from the local government, rely on these verified pages:

1. **Biñan City Information Office (CIO) Facebook Page:**
   - The primary source for Mayoral announcements, class and work suspensions (Walang Pasok), and city-wide alerts.
2. **Biñan City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO) Facebook Page:**
   - Provides real-time weather monitoring, river water level updates, and typhoon tracking.

---

## Early Warning Systems (EWS)

The CDRRMO employs both technological and community-based systems to alert residents of impending dangers.

**1. River and Flood Monitoring**
The C3 continually monitors the water levels of major waterways traversing the city, particularly the Biñan River.

- During continuous heavy rainfall, the CDRRMO issues status updates (Normal, Alert, Alarm, Critical).
- Sirens and public address systems installed in flood-prone barangays are activated to signal preemptive or forced evacuation.

**2. CCTV Surveillance**
The Biñan C3 monitors a city-wide network of high-definition CCTV cameras to spot traffic accidents, flash floods, and public safety incidents in real time, allowing for rapid dispatch without waiting for a civilian call.

**3. Barangay DRRM Paging Systems**
Barangay officials use local roaming vehicles with megaphones (bandilyo) and stationary paging systems to announce localized warnings and evacuation orders directly to neighborhoods.

---

## National Warning Agencies

The City of Biñan bases its localized alerts on data from these national agencies. You should also monitor them directly during extreme weather:

- **PAGASA (DOST-PAGASA):** For Tropical Cyclone Bulletins, Rainfall Advisories, and severe weather updates.
- **PHIVOLCS:** For earthquake bulletins and volcanic activity reports.
- **NDRRMC:** For nationwide mobile emergency alerts (text blasts) regarding severe weather systems.
  _(Ensure your smartphone's "Emergency Alerts / Cell Broadcast" settings are turned on to receive NDRRMC text warnings)._
