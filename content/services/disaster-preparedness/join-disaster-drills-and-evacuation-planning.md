# Join Disaster Drills and Evacuation Planning — City of Biñan

The **Biñan City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)** regularly conducts drills and capacity-building programs to ensure communities, schools, and businesses are prepared for emergencies such as earthquakes, fires, and typhoons.

---

## Nationwide Simultaneous Earthquake Drill (NSED)

Biñan City actively participates in the quarterly NSED.

- **Who should join:** All public and private schools, barangay halls, business establishments, and City Hall employees.
- **What to do:** Practice the **"Duck, Cover, and Hold"** technique and execute the building's specific evacuation plan to the designated open area.
- **Evaluation:** CDRRMO personnel are deployed across various sectors to evaluate evacuation times and proper protocols.

## BIDA Kids: Batang First Aid Responder

The CDRRMO runs the _BIDA Kids_ program aimed at teaching young students basic life support and emergency response.

- **Target Audience:** Elementary and high school students in Biñan.
- **Focus:** Basic first aid, emergency contact protocols, and personal safety during disasters.
- **How to request:** School administrators can send a formal request letter to the CDRRMO for scheduling.

## Community and Corporate Training

Barangays, homeowner associations, and private businesses can request localized disaster management training.

**Available Modules:**

- Basic Incident Command System (BICS)
- Standard First Aid & Basic Life Support (CPR)
- Fire Use and Extinguisher Training (in coordination with BFP Biñan)
- Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction and Management (BDRRM) Planning

**How to Request Training:**

1. Draft a formal letter addressed to the **Head of the CDRRMO**.
2. Specify the requested training module, target date, venue, and estimated number of participants.
3. Submit the letter to the CDRRMO at the Biñan Command and Control Center (C3) or via email at cdrrmo@binan.gov.ph.
