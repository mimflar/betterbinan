# Emergency Response Procedures and Evacuation — City of Biñan

Being prepared and knowing exactly who to call and where to go can save lives. The City of Biñan operates a centralized 24/7 **Command and Control Center (C3)** to dispatch medical, fire, and rescue units immediately.

---

## 24/7 Biñan Emergency Hotlines

Save these numbers in your phone. Do not hesitate to call during medical emergencies, fires, vehicular accidents, or severe flooding.

- **Smart / TNT:** 0968-185-8911 / 0908-891-9711
- **Globe / TM:** 0917-120-8911
- **PLDT:** (049) 536-2622 / (049) 523-5911
- **Intelco:** (049) 513-9111

**When calling the C3 Dispatcher:**

1. State your name and exact location (Barangay, Street, Landmark).
2. Describe the emergency clearly and calmly.
3. State the number of injured persons, if any.
4. Do not hang up until the dispatcher tells you to.

## Flood Preparedness & Evacuation Procedures

Because parts of Biñan are situated near Laguna de Bay, certain barangays are prone to flooding during heavy monsoon rains (Habagat) and typhoons.

**Pre-Evacuation (Alert Level):**

- Secure important documents in waterproof containers.
- Move appliances and furniture to higher ground or a second floor.
- Prepare your "Go Bag" (survival kit with 3 days of food, water, medicine, flashlights, and radios).

**During Evacuation:**

- Follow the instructions of your **Barangay DRRM Council**. If preemptive evacuation is ordered, comply immediately.
- Turn off the main electrical switch and water valve before leaving your home.
- Use safe, designated routes determined by your barangay to avoid deep floodwaters or strong currents.

**Designated Evacuation Centers:**
Evacuation centers vary by barangay but typically include:

- Public Elementary and High Schools
- Barangay Covered Courts
- Alonte Sports Arena (for large-scale city-wide evacuations)

_Contact your local Barangay Hall to confirm your specific designated evacuation center._

---

## Fire Emergencies

1. Alert everyone in the building and evacuate immediately.
2. Call the Biñan C3 Emergency Hotline or the **Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) Biñan**.
3. Do not attempt to save belongings if the fire is spreading rapidly.
4. Crawl low under smoke to escape.
