# Apply for Socialized Housing or Relocation — City of Biñan

The City Government of Biñan, through the **Urban Development and Housing Office (UDHO)**, coordinates with national agencies like the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD) and the Social Housing Finance Corporation (SHFC) to provide secure, affordable, and permanent homes for low-income families and informal settler families (ISFs).

---

## Socialized Housing Programs in Biñan

Biñan City actively implements the **Expanded Pambansang Pabahay para sa Pilipino (4PH) Program** and the **Enhanced Community Mortgage Program (CMP)** to help minimum-wage earners, delivery riders, and long-term informal settlers achieve land tenure and homeownership.

**Examples of Local Projects:**

- **The Meadow (4PH Program):** Condominium-style socialized housing units designed to provide safe vertical homeownership for low-income families.
- **Southville 5-A Housing Project:** Located in Brgy. Langkiwa, serving as a primary relocation site for displaced families and calamity victims.
- **Community Mortgage Programs (e.g., Dreamland HOA in Brgy. Tubigan):** Government-backed land acquisition for communities that have occupied land for decades, allowing them to legally secure their tenure.

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Informal Settler Families (ISFs)** living in danger zones (e.g., riverbanks, under transmission lines, or right-of-way areas).
- Low-income residents who do not own any real property.
- Members of accredited Homeowners Associations (HOAs) seeking land tenure through the Community Mortgage Program.
- Victims of natural disasters or court-ordered demolitions.

## How to Apply

1. **Organize or Join a Homeowners Association (HOA):**
   - For community-level housing applications (like the CMP), residents must form or join an LGU-recognized HOA.
2. **Visit the UDHO:**
   - Proceed to the **Urban Development and Housing Office** at the Biñan City Hall.
   - Submit a letter of intent to avail of the socialized housing program.
3. **Submit Initial Requirements (for profiling):**
   - Proof of Biñan Residency (Barangay Certificate / Voter's Certification).
   - PSA Birth Certificate and Marriage Certificate (if applicable).
   - Proof of Income (Payslip, Certificate of Employment, or Certificate of Indigency if unemployed).
   - Affidavit of No Property Ownership.
4. **Validation and Profiling:**
   - The UDHO, alongside the SHFC, will conduct a background check and census tagging to verify your eligibility for the requested housing project.
