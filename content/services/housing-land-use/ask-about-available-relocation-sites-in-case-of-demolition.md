# Relocation Sites in Case of Demolition — City of Biñan

The City Government of Biñan adheres strictly to the Urban Development and Housing Act (UDHA), ensuring that informal settler families (ISFs) affected by lawful evictions, infrastructure projects, or natural disasters are provided with adequate relocation options.

---

## When is Relocation Provided?

The city prioritizes relocation assistance for ISFs facing displacement due to:

- **Danger Zone Clearing:** Families living along the Biñan River, esteros, railroad tracks, or under transmission lines.
- **Government Infrastructure Projects:** Displacement caused by national or local road widening and public works.
- **Court-Ordered Demolitions:** Legal evictions from private property where the LGU is mandated to provide resettlement assistance.
- **Natural Calamities:** Families displaced by severe typhoons, flooding, or fires.

## Designated Relocation Sites

The city coordinates with the National Housing Authority (NHA) and the Social Housing Finance Corporation (SHFC) to utilize local resettlement areas, such as:

- **Southville 5-A Housing Project (Brgy. Langkiwa):** A primary government relocation site utilized for displaced families (including victims of major typhoons like Ondoy).
- **In-City Socialized Housing:** The city is transitioning toward vertical housing (condominium-style units like _The Meadow_ under the 4PH program) to keep relocated families close to their livelihoods in Biñan.

## What to Do During a Notice of Eviction / Demolition

1. **Do not ignore the notice:** If you receive a formal Notice of Eviction or Demolition, immediately coordinate with your Barangay Chairman.
2. **Proceed to the UDHO / CSWDO:** Visit the **Urban Development and Housing Office** and the **City Social Welfare and Development Office** at the Biñan City Hall.
3. **Undergo Census and Tagging:** The UDHO conducts a pre-relocation census to verify that you are the actual occupant of the structure. **Only tagged residents are eligible for relocation packages.**
4. **Attend the Pre-Demolition Conference (PDC):** The LGU, the Presidential Commission for the Urban Poor (PCUP), and the affected residents will meet to discuss the timeline, the dismantling process, and the specific relocation site assigned to your community.

---

## Financial and Transport Assistance

During the actual relocation day, the City Government usually provides:

- **Transportation (Hakot) Assistance:** City trucks to move your belongings to the new site.
- **Food Assistance:** Relief packs provided by the CSWDO during the transfer.
- **Financial Assistance (Balik-Probinsya):** For families who prefer to return to their home provinces instead of moving to the local relocation site, the CSWDO may provide transportation and subsistence allowances.
