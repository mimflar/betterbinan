# Request Zoning, Land Use, or Building Permits — City of Biñan

Before constructing, renovating, or occupying a commercial or residential building in Biñan, you must secure a **Locational/Zoning Clearance** and a **Building Permit** to ensure your structure complies with the National Building Code of the Philippines and the city's Comprehensive Land Use Plan.

---

## 1) Locational / Zoning Clearance

Secured **before** applying for a Building Permit or a Business Permit. This ensures your intended building or business activity matches the designated zone (e.g., you cannot build a factory in a strictly residential zone).

**Where to apply:** City Planning and Development Office (CPDO)

**Basic Requirements:**

- Duly accomplished Application Form for Locational Clearance.
- Barangay Clearance for construction or business.
- Proof of Ownership (Transfer Certificate of Title / TCT) or Notarized Lease Contract.
- Updated Tax Declaration and Real Property Tax (RPT) Receipt.
- Lot Plan and Vicinity Map.

---

## 2) Building Permit Application

Required for new constructions, major repairs, renovations, or demolition of any structure.

**Where to apply:** Office of the Building Official (OBO) / City Engineering Office

**Basic Requirements:**

- **Approved Locational/Zoning Clearance** (from CPDO).
- Duly accomplished Building Permit Application Forms (signed and sealed by a licensed Architect or Civil Engineer).
- Complete set of Building Plans (Architectural, Structural, Sanitary/Plumbing, Electrical, Mechanical) – signed and sealed.
- Bill of Materials and Cost Estimates.
- Structural Design Computations (for structures 2 stories and above).
- Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) from the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) Biñan.
- Proof of Property Ownership (TCT, Tax Declaration).

---

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Secure Zoning Clearance (CPDO):**
   - Submit your lot plan and proof of ownership to the CPDO at City Hall. Pay the corresponding zoning fees at the City Treasurer's Office to claim your clearance.
2. **Submit Building Permit Documents (OBO):**
   - Proceed to the City Engineering Office / OBO. Submit your signed/sealed plans, zoning clearance, and FSEC.
3. **Assessment and Routing:**
   - The OBO will route your plans to different divisions (Architectural, Structural, Electrical, Sanitary) for code compliance checking.
4. **Payment of Fees:**
   - Once the plans are approved, the OBO will issue an Order of Payment. Pay the building permit fees at the City Treasurer's Office.
5. **Release of Permit:**
   - Present your Official Receipt to the OBO to claim your Building Permit. **You must post the permit conspicuously at your construction site.**

---

## Important Reminders

- **Ancillary Permits:** Depending on your project, you may also need separate Electrical, Plumbing, or Mechanical Permits.
- **Certificate of Occupancy:** After construction is finished, you **cannot** legally occupy the building or operate a business in it until the OBO inspects the finished structure and issues a Certificate of Occupancy.
- **Location:** Both the CPDO and the City Engineering Office/OBO are located at the **Biñan City Hall Compound, Brgy. Zapote**.
